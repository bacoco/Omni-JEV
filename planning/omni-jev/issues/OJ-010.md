# [P1] Evaluate audiovisual relationships and temporal counterfactuals

**Planning ID:** `OJ-010`  
**Priority:** P1  
**Status:** Proposed; no experiment results yet.

## Goal

Distinguish matching topics from matching events and precise timing. Confirm that joint inputs are actually used rather than answered from one modality or from editing artifacts.

## Scope

- Build audio-only, video-only, transcript-only, and jointly aligned conditions with matched source content.
- Create controlled time offsets and event-order inversions; document sampling resolution and intended tolerance.
- Include semantically matched but temporally wrong streams, contradictory speech/visual evidence, and missing/muted audio.
- Balance edit artifacts, inspect samples, and keep all edits of one source in the same split.
- Run both semantic and synchrony questions on the same content so timing-insensitive and timing-sensitive behavior can be distinguished.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The suite records which offsets are representable under the actual preprocessing.
- [ ] Modality-ablation and temporal-sensitivity results are available for baselines and later selected heads.
- [ ] Unsupported precision is declared rather than inferred from high coarse retrieval scores.
- [ ] Evidence unavailability and true negative decisions are scored separately.

A concise implementation/experiment report, pinned revisions, commands, results or failure reasons, and any changed assumptions. Do not present the plan itself as a successful run.
